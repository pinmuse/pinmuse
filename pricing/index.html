Good to know

How does billing work?

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID or Google Play account — monthly or annually, whichever you choose. It renews automatically until you turn it off.

What happens when I use up my free styling suggestions?

Nothing is lost — your wardrobe and past matches stay exactly as they are. You can upgrade to Premium anytime to keep getting new suggestions, or simply wait for your next rolling 30-day period.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in your device's account settings whenever you like — you'll keep Premium access until the end of the period you already paid for, with no further charges after that.

Is there a free trial?

You don't need one — every account starts with 10 free styling suggestions in the first 30 days, roughly double the usual free allowance, so you can try real matches before deciding.
